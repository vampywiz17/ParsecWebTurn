ICE route describes the selected candidate pair. Configuring TURN does not force relay. Traffic is measured usage; RTT is not an ICMP ping. FPS counts decoded video frames, not screen refreshes. Video packet loss is reported only when RTP statistics expose it; data-channel video has no equivalent loss counter. WebRTC encryption is DTLS between peers, separate from TLS to a TURN server. Data channels use DTLS. A reported SRTP profile describes the negotiated profile; it does not establish that RTP media is being sent. Missing encryption details are unknown, not proof of an unencrypted connection.

Audio configuration remains visible during silence. Configured audio bitrate is not measured traffic: the receiving WebCodecs/WebRTC APIs do not expose the remote encoder target, so it remains Not reported.
